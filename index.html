The Seven
Constants

Autonomy is governed not by promises, but by what remains true.

Seven control invariants define the conditions under which autonomous AI remains under accountable human control.

The premise

A safety rule inside the model is a request. A Constant is a property of the system around it.

I

Observable Agency

Consequential autonomous action remains observable.

II

Mediated Action

MED-001 — MED-006
III

Revocable Authority

Granted authority remains externally revocable.

REV-001 — REV-006
IV

Protected Oversight

Oversight remains outside the actor’s control.

Bounded Delegation
