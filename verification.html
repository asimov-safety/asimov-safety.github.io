01

Exact artifact

Changing even one byte of the public report changes its SHA-256 digest and breaks the report-to-statement match.

02

Authenticated issuer

When Sigstore verifies, the exact statement was signed by the expected OIDC-backed identity rather than merely carrying an editable name field.

03

External checkpoint

Sigstore's transparency infrastructure provides an external, append-only checkpoint so the signer cannot silently rewrite the historical commitment later.

04

Semantic limits

A valid signature proves provenance and binding—not that every finding is correct. Required human and independent review remains part of the assessment result itself.
