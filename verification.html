Verification

Evidence must
survive belief.

Asimov separates four questions that are often collapsed into one: are the bytes intact, who committed to them, when were they anchored, and do they actually support the claim?

Content integrity

Deterministic manifests bind relied-upon artifacts and results to cryptographic digests.

02

Signer identity

Authenticated assessment identities bind themselves to the evidence commitment and assessed scope.

03

External checkpoint

A transparency log, trusted timestamp, or equivalent append-only mechanism anchors the commitment outside the assessed actor.

04

Semantic assurance

Independent review establishes that the evidence supports the control property being claimed. Cryptography cannot substitute for this step.

From assessment to verification

Know what is required before you sign.

The Verify form checks a finished package. It does not create the human review, evidence integrity boundary, signer identity, external checkpoint, or independent A5 assurance for you.

A1+

Trusted evidence integrity

ACC-002 applies from A1. Build the evidence manifest, verify it, and retain trusted integrity state outside the actor's unauthorized mutation authority. A local hash file stored beside actor-writable evidence is not, by itself, an independent trust anchor.

A4

Authenticated signing + external checkpoint

ACC-005 requires deterministic artifact binding, an authenticated assessment identity, and an external transparency/timestamp/append-only or equivalent independent checkpoint. The generated workflow recommends Sigstore/Cosign and requires the exact expected identity and OIDC issuer for verification.

A5

Independent assurance + escrow

ACC-006 additionally requires an independent assessment, durable evidence retention/escrow outside the assessed actor and ordinary mutable operator path, and successful reverification from a fresh environment. A self-review cannot satisfy an independence-required family.
