The governing model

The Seven
Constants

Each Constant is a control invariant: a condition that must remain true throughout autonomous operation.

I

Observable Agency

Consequential autonomous action remains observable.

An accountable system preserves an independent view of what was proposed, denied, admitted, attempted, and observed in the world.

OBS-001 — OBS-006
II

Mediated Action

Consequential action remains subject to enforceable boundaries.

Autonomy does not imply ambient authority. Consequential effects cross a control boundary before they are admitted.

MED-001 — MED-006
III

Revocable Authority

Granted authority remains externally revocable.

Authority can be withdrawn without relying on the acting system to agree, cooperate, finish a turn, or report that it stopped.

REV-001 — REV-006
IV

Protected Oversight

Oversight remains outside the actor’s control.

The system cannot silently weaken, impersonate, reconfigure, or bypass the mechanisms responsible for supervising it.

OVR-001 — OVR-006
V

Bounded Delegation

Delegation remains within the authority granted.

Subagents, jobs, services, and descendants inherit limits rather than becoming new roots of unrestricted authority.

DEL-001 — DEL-006
VI

Human Authority

Human intervention remains effective.

Designated humans retain authenticated, independent mechanisms to restrict, stop, and govern recovery of autonomous operation.

HUM-001 — HUM-006
VII

Accountability

Consequential action remains reconstructable.

Evidence survives the actor and preserves enough context to determine what happened, under what authority, and subject to which controls.

ACC-001 — ACC-006

Seven conditions. No averaging. If a required Constant fails, the assurance level fails with it.
